ADMINPlatform owner / global control

[01] GLOBAL SCOPE

Admin

Controls the platform and its operational layer.

  • Resellers and clients
  • FusionPBX servers
  • Caller profiles and deposits
  • Call records and queue health
[02] TENANT SCOPE

Reseller

Runs a branded workspace for its own customers.

  • Brand name, logo, domain
  • Client accounts and balances
  • Campaigns and reports
  • Support identity
[03] OWNED SCOPE

Client

Owns the call identity and campaign activity.

  • Caller ID and SIP profile
  • Contacts and campaigns
  • Outbound calls and status
  • Balance and reports
DATA BOUNDARY
Every query resolves ownership from the authenticated user. Client A cannot read Client B; a reseller sees only its child clients; SIP passwords stay encrypted and never enter the browser or queue payload.
